Who We Are

A growing Nigerian agribusiness, built with farmers in mind

Newman's Agribusiness is a Nigerian agribusiness enterprise built around one idea: that agriculture can feed the nation and empower the people who work the land. Since beginning operations in 2021, we have worked to bring quality agro-inputs, practical knowledge and market opportunities closer to the farmers who need them most.

We position ourselves as an Agro-Input Supply and Farmer Support Hub — supplying certified seeds, fertilizers, herbicides and tools, while also walking with farmers through training, extension support and produce aggregation. We serve and empower people irrespective of religion, ethnicity, gender or community background.

We are a young company, still growing, still learning, and committed to building something credible and lasting for Nigerian agriculture.

What We Do

Value across the agricultural chain

From the seed in the ground to the produce at market, our work spans inputs, farming, training and market access.

Agro-Input Supply

Certified seeds, fertilizers, herbicides, sprayers, cutlasses, hoes and other essential farming tools.

Maize Starter Pack

Seeds, fertilizer, herbicide and guidance bundled together for easier, more productive planting.

Crop Farming

Direct cultivation that keeps us close to the realities and seasons that our farmers work with.

Poultry Farming

Poultry operations that add to local food supply and diversify our agribusiness activities.

Farmer Training

Practical, hands-on extension support covering modern and climate-smart farming practices.

Post-Harvest Support

Guidance on storage, preservation and handling to reduce losses after the harvest.

Produce Aggregation

Bringing farmer produce together to improve bargaining power and access to buyers.

Market Linkage

Connecting farmers to buyers and off-takers so productivity translates into real income.

Flagship Product

The Maize Starter Pack

Our flagship solution brings the essentials of maize farming together in one package — so farmers spend less time sourcing inputs separately, and more time growing. Every pack comes with basic guidance on proper use, so farmers get the most from what they plant.

  • Certified maize seeds
  • Appropriate fertilizer
  • Recommended herbicide
  • Basic farming guidance
